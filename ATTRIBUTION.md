# Media attribution

The catalog links to open films hosted by [Internet Archive](https://archive.org) and to the CC0 flower example maintained by [MDN Web Docs](https://developer.mozilla.org/). Each Internet Archive detail page identifies its original creator and applicable license.

Local thumbnails and three-second preview excerpts were generated from their matching source media for this prototype. Blender open-movie titles and artwork belong to their respective Blender Foundation or Blender Studio production teams.

The Kinet name, interface, icons, and visual system were created for this prototype. Platform names and marks belong to their respective owners.
